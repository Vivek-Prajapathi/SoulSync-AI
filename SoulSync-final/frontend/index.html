A SIMPLE PRACTICE, MADE CLEARER

From mat to feedback
in three steps

01

Frame yourself

Prop your phone or laptop about two metres away so your whole body is in view, head to feet.

02

Pick a pose

Each pose comes with its target angles and a one-line cue, so you know what good looks like before you start.

03

Hold and listen

The hold timer only runs while your form is right. Cues appear one at a time, so you are never reading a checklist mid-pose.
